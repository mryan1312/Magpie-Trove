Magpie Trove — Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

Magpie Trove is a desktop application for tagging, searching and organising images. It runs entirely on your computer.

What we collect

Nothing. Magpie Trove has no accounts, no sign-in, no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. No information about you, your computer or your images is transmitted to the developer or to anyone else.

Your images and tags

Everything the app creates — the tag database, thumbnails, settings and the visual-search index — is written to your own computer and stays there:

  • Installed from the Microsoft Store: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\Meryndi.MagpieTrove_8xr7vqtychad0\LocalCache\Local\MagpieTrove\
  • Installed from the portable download: %LOCALAPPDATA%\MagpieTrove\
  • Libraries you create in another folder are stored in that folder.

Your image files themselves are never uploaded. They are read, not modified, and Magpie Trove will not move, alter or delete any file except where you explicitly ask it to: the export and transfer features write new files, and Delete rejects sends the files you have flagged as rejected to the Windows Recycle Bin after asking you to confirm. Nothing is ever deleted permanently by the app, and nothing is deleted without that confirmation.

Network use

Magpie Trove makes exactly one kind of outbound request, and only when you ask it to.

Visual search — finding lookalike images and suggesting tags — needs a machine-learning model that is too large to ship inside the app. When you choose Libraries… → Download model, the app downloads that model file over HTTPS from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) and verifies it against a published checksum before use.

That request sends no personal data — no images, no tags, no filenames, no identifiers. It is an ordinary file download. As with visiting any website, the server you download from will see your IP address and the request itself; that is handled under Hugging Face's privacy policy, not ours. If you never download the model, Magpie Trove makes no network requests at all, and the rest of the app works normally without it.

Children

Magpie Trove is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

Your data, your control

Because nothing is collected or transmitted, there is no data held about you to access, correct, export or erase. You can delete everything the app has stored at any time by uninstalling it and deleting the folders listed above.
